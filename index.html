How it works

Three steps, no spreadsheets

NicheForge AI does the two things a content creator actually needs before filming: confirm real demand exists, and find the specific angle nobody's nailed yet.

1. You name a topic

Broad or specific — "sourdough for beginners" or "budget home espresso setups" both work.

2. We pull real YouTube data

Views, upload cadence, channel size, and any breakout videos from the last 12 months — via YouTube's own API, not a scraped guess.

3. Your AI writes the strategy

Your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini key turns the real data into ranked angles, a content gap, and title ideas — grounded in what's actually there.

Why trust the data

Every number is labeled — real or estimated

Most "niche finder" tools show you a score and ask you to trust it. NicheForge AI shows its work.

Comes straight from YouTube's API

  • Average and median views across the sample
  • Share of channels with 100k+ subscribers
  • Uploads per week and days since the last upload
  • Breakout videos that beat their own channel's average by 2x+

Computed by a fixed, visible formula

  • Opportunity Score = a demand component, minus a saturation penalty, plus a freshness bonus
  • The exact formula is shown with every scan — nothing is a black box
  • AI is only allowed to reason from these real numbers, never invent new ones
  • If the AI's response doesn't match the required format, you see the raw output instead of a fabricated result

FAQ

Common questions

Why do I need my own AI key?

It keeps NicheForge AI's price low and one-time instead of a monthly subscription — you only pay your AI provider for what you actually use, usually a fraction of a cent per scan.

Is my API key stored anywhere?

No. Everything in Settings — your AI key and, if you add one, your own YouTube Data API key — is kept in your browser's local storage and sent only with each scan request to run that one scan. Neither is ever written to our database.

Do I need my own YouTube API key?

No — the app uses a shared key by default so most people never touch this. If you want to use your own free Google quota instead (useful for heavy testing), add it in Settings; it's entirely optional.

What exactly is "real" versus "estimated"?

Views, subscriber counts, upload dates, and the Opportunity Score come directly from YouTube's API through a fixed formula shown on every result. The ranked angles, title ideas, and content gap are AI-written interpretations of that real data — useful judgment calls, not guaranteed outcomes.

Does a high Opportunity Score guarantee views?

No tool can promise that, and we won't claim otherwise. It's a research signal that shows where demand looks strong relative to competition right now — execution still matters.
