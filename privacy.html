NicheForge AI (“NicheForge,” “we,” “us”) is a content-research tool for video and content marketers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. NicheForge AI is operated by [Your business name / legal entity]. Questions? Contact [your support email].

1. Information you provide directly

  • Your email address — collected when you run a scan (to unlock your free scans and, if you purchase, to deliver your license key) or contact us.
  • A license key, if you purchase one or already hold one.
  • Your own API keys, if you choose to connect your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or YouTube Data API key (“BYOK”). These are stored only in your own browser’s local storage and are sent directly from your browser to the relevant provider, or — when you use our shared YouTube quota instead of your own key — to our server solely to make that one request. We do not keep a copy of your API keys on our servers.
  • Payment information, if you purchase a license, is collected and processed entirely by PayPal. We never receive or store your card or bank details — PayPal shares with us only what's needed to activate your license (confirmation that payment succeeded, and an order/transaction ID).

2. Information generated by using the tool

  • The topics, video URLs, channel URLs, and transcripts you submit for analysis, and the AI-generated results.
  • If you save items to your History, Watchlist, Reports, tracked Competitors, or Playbooks, we store those records, tied to the email you provided, so you can return to them later.
  • Basic usage information needed to enforce free-scan limits (a count of scans used, tied to your email).

3. Information from YouTube API Services

NicheForge uses YouTube API Services to retrieve real, publicly available data — video titles, view counts, channel statistics, public comments, and similar public metadata — in response to the topics and URLs you submit. We do not ask you to sign in with a Google or YouTube account, and we do not access any private YouTube data on your behalf.

By using NicheForge, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your use of YouTube data through this tool is additionally subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How we use this information

  • To run the scans and analyses you request, and show you the results.
  • To enforce free-scan limits and validate license keys.
  • To deliver your license key and purchase confirmation after a purchase.
  • To save your History, Watchlist, Reports, and other saved items so you can return to them.
  • To send you scan results, license delivery, and — if you don't opt out — occasional product updates.
  • To detect and prevent abuse of free-scan limits.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Third parties involved

  • PayPal processes payments for paid licenses. Their handling of your payment data is governed by PayPal’s own privacy policy.
  • Your chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini) processes the evidence NicheForge sends it, using your own API key, to generate analysis. That provider’s own privacy policy and terms govern how they handle that data — this is your own account and key, so we don’t control it.
  • Google / YouTube API Services provides the real video, channel, and comment data NicheForge analyzes. See Section 3 above.
  • Netlify hosts NicheForge and stores the data described above on our behalf.

6. Data storage and retention

Saved scans, watchlist items, reports, tracked competitors, and playbooks are retained until you ask us to delete them, or until we discontinue the relevant feature. Usage counts and license records are retained to enforce our license terms.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can clear your browser’s local storage at any time to remove your saved AI/YouTube keys and settings from your device.
  • You can ask us to delete the data associated with your email address by contacting [your support email]. We’ll act on that request within a reasonable time.
  • If you’re located somewhere with specific statutory privacy rights (for example, the EU/UK or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing — contact us and we’ll do our best to accommodate applicable requirements.

8. Cookies and local storage

NicheForge stores your settings (AI provider, license key, score-weight preferences, and similar) in your browser’s local storage, not cookies. This data stays on your device and is only sent to our server as part of the specific requests you make, such as running a scan.

9. Children

NicheForge is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We’ll update the “Last updated” date above when we do.
